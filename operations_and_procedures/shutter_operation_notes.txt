====================================
*** INITIAL FILL OF LOW PRESSURE TANK
====================================
1)  Initial Fill of Low Pressure Tank:
    a)  Ask PBS-31 to supply gass
    b)  Open VG-0323 and VG-0317
    c)  Monitor MP-0004 until gas pressure in the Low Pressure take is stabilized
    d)  Close VG-0323 and VG-0317

===================
*** Closing Shutter
===================
2)  Fill High Pressure Tank:
    a)  Check that all valves are closed
    b)  Open VG-0315, VG-0317, and VG-0301
    C)  Start compressor
    d)  Monitor MP-0002 until gas pressure in the High Pressure tank is stabilized
    e)  Close all valves
    f)  Stop compressor

3)  Enabling Shutter Closure:
    a)  Open VG-3014
    b)  Ask PBS-48 to open SIC valves (VG-0321, and VG-0322)

4)  Closing the Shutter:
    a)  Open VG-0301
    b)  Monitor the shutter status until it is closed
    c)  Close VG-3014
    d)  Ask PBS-48 to close SIC valves (VG-0321, and VG-0322)
    e)  Close VG-301?

===================
*** Opening Shutter
===================
1)  Enabling Shutter Opening:
    a)  Open VG-3014
    b)  Ask PBS-48 to open SIC valves (VG-0321, and VG-0322)

2)  Opening Shutter:
    a)  Open VG-0319
    b)  Monitor MP-0002 and MP-0004 until gas pressure in the Low Pressure tank
        is stabilized
    c)  Monitor the shutter status until it is open
    d)  Close VG-0219
    e)  Close VG-3014
    f)  Ask PBS-48 to close SIC valves (VG-0321, and VG-0322)

===========================
*** CLEAR LOW PRESSURE TANK
===========================
1)  Clear Low Pressure tank:
    a)  Ask PBS-31 for a vacuum in the pipe
    b)  Open VG-0317, and VG-0323
    c)  Monitor MP-0001 and MP-0004 until they show that the vacuum has
        stabilized
    d)  Close VG-0317 and VG-0323
    e)  Reset the request to PBS-31
